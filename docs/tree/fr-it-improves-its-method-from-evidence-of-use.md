mechanism: |-
  Stage 3. A problem that recurs in two branches or more is a method problem; the consolidator
  proposes a change; a replay battery of past real cases, the misses included, decides; a change
  of how is adopted and rolled back automatically, one of what or of the evaluator is the owner's;
  every record carries the method version; the evidence comes from the products, never from
  Agentos's own development.
---
Agentos changes how it works when the evidence of its products being used says so, never
at the cost of what it already does well. That evidence comes from the products, not from
Agentos's own development.
