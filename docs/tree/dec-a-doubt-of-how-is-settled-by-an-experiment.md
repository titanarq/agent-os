type: decision
title: A doubt of how is settled by an experiment, never by a question to the owner
state: in-force
decided: '2026-10-06'
sources:
- Owner design discussion of 2026-10-06, how pass, level 3, goal C (the owner decides what; Agentos decides how)
- 'Owner design discussion of 2026-10-05/06, point 3 (questions: how against what, defaults, sessions)'
premises:
- 'When the goal is clear, choosing between two ways of meeting it is mechanical: try, measure, keep the better'
- The owner's attention is the most expensive experiment there is
- Classifying a doubt as how wrongly lets the what leak into a decision nobody sees, so the classification itself needs a check
rejected_alternatives:
- option: Ask the owner every doubt
  reason: It spends the owner's attention on what evidence can settle
  basis: stated
- option: A requirement of its own that gates every delegated judgment by measured accuracy
  reason: 'The owner removed it on 2026-10-06: the validator''s calibration (goal A) and the custodian''s record (goal B) already cover it; the improvement loop lives in goal D'
  basis: stated
review_triggers:
- The owner reclaims, in question sessions, a share of the decisions taken without them that does not fall over time
---
Every question carries its scope. A doubt of **how** goes to the expert and, if the expert cannot
settle it, to an experiment (a spike, or alternatives in parallel when the indicators ask for it);
it never reaches the owner. A doubt of **what** goes to a question session with a default answer;
until it is answered it blocks the hardening of its node, which meanwhile stays schematic.
Every question session carries a short digest of what was decided without the owner: anything the
owner reclaims becomes a question of what, is counted against whoever classified it, and that kind
of doubt goes to the owner until the method that classifies it changes.
