- The feedback interpreter marks every item with its `origin` (`stated`, `derived` or `detected`) and code sets the status from it for `change`, `decision`, `puntal_change` and `verdict`: stated and detected are born `approved`, derived is born `proposed` and is asked about in the chat while the rest goes on; a detected item (a clash with a goal or an evaluator above the case, listed in the brief) is applied and commented as an already-accepted question (#203).
- `sessions.confirm_before_dispatch` is now the review mode and defaults to false: on, every item is born `proposed` as before; off, `test-ingest` launches everything but the items marked `proposed`. A `puntal_change` is no longer always proposed, and `prompts/interpreter.md` teaches the three origins (#203).
