- The integrator's judge: `prompts/integrator.md` and its one-turn driver `bin/integrator_task.sh judge` (class `integrator`, no tool, an index and no diff) answer, per group the rules allowed, `independent`, `unsure` or `coupled` with a one-line reason, and may only confirm, split or send solo. `agent-os-integrate plan --judge` builds the index (title, closed issue's title, files, the test groups of the plan, up to 40 hunk headers, the union and largest-member test counts), validates that the answer partitions the group, and prints the final groups with the reasons. An unparsable answer, a timeout, an exhausted quota or a refused config leaves every pull request alone; `integrator.judge: false` keeps the group by the mechanical rules alone. The rule modules moved to `agent_os/product/integrator/rules/` to make room.
